# Tax Filing Preparation

## Two different things wear the same word

A **rule** is what the statute says. A **position** is what you argue when the statute does not clearly say. Conflating them is how a filing becomes indefensible: the reviewer discovers the confident sentence was an interpretation.

State which is which. "Deductible under s.37" and "we treat this as revenue expenditure because the benefit does not extend beyond the year" are different sentences with different risks.

## The deadline is not the constraint people think

Late filing is a known, priced penalty. An incorrect filing is an open liability with interest, and it resets attention on prior years. Where you cannot get a number right in time, prefer the extension or the estimated payment over the confident guess.

## Reconcile the return to the accounts

Every figure on a return should trace to a ledger balance plus a named adjustment. Book-to-tax differences are the whole substance of the work: depreciation, provisions, disallowed expenses, timing.

A return that cannot be tied back to the trial balance will not survive the first question about it.

## Never state a rate or a deadline from memory

Thresholds, rates and due dates change, and they change by jurisdiction and by entity type. Look them up for the specific year and entity, every time, and cite where you looked.

This is the single most common way tax work goes wrong, and it is entirely avoidable.

## Documentation is the deliverable

The file should let a different practitioner reach the same numbers next year. That means the workings, the source documents, the positions taken and the reason for each — not just the final schedule.

## Watch out for

- Related-party transactions without documented arm's-length reasoning.
- Personal expenditure routed through the business, which is where most disputes begin.
- Prior-year adjustments repeated because nobody read last year's file.
- Cash-basis figures used in an accrual return.
- A position taken because it was taken last year, with no one able to say why.

## Finishing

Every number traces to the accounts. Positions are labelled as positions with reasoning. Rates and dates were verified for this year, not recalled.
