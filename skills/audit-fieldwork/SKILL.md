# Audit Fieldwork

## Evidence has a hierarchy, and it is not a preference

External confirmation beats a system report; a system report beats a client-prepared schedule; a client-prepared schedule beats a verbal explanation. Substituting downward without saying so is the most common defect in a working paper.

When you accept weaker evidence, record why the stronger evidence was unavailable.

## Test the control or test the balance, and know which

Controls testing asks whether the process would catch an error. Substantive testing asks whether this number is right. They answer different questions, and a controls conclusion cannot be repaired by more substantive work at the same sample size.

If controls fail, the substantive sample gets larger. That is the actual consequence, not a note in the file.

## Sampling is a claim about a population

A sample is only meaningful with a stated population, a stated method and a stated basis for the size. "Twenty invoices" is not a sample; "twenty of 4,300 payments over the threshold, selected by monetary-unit sampling" is.

Judgemental selection is legitimate and must be labelled as such, because it supports no statistical extrapolation.

## A finding needs cause, effect and extent

"Three purchase orders lacked approval" is an observation. The finding is what allowed it, what it could cost, and how much of the population is likely affected. Without extent, management cannot size the response and will reasonably discount it.

## Watch out for

- Rolling forward last year's programme without asking what changed.
- Evidence obtained from the person whose work is being tested, unverified.
- Journal entries posted outside the normal cycle or by unusual users — the highest-yield test in most audits.
- Immaterial findings crowding out a material one in the report.
- Confirming the balance you expected and stopping there.

## Finishing

Every conclusion names its evidence and its evidence's tier. Samples state population, method and size basis. Findings carry cause, effect and extent.
