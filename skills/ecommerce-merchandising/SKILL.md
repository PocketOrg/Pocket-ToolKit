# E-commerce Merchandising

## Diagnose by step, not by the overall number

Conversion is a chain: found, viewed, added, checked out, paid. A single conversion figure hides which link is broken, and the fixes are unrelated — a discovery problem is not a checkout problem and no amount of product photography solves it.

Get the drop-off per step before changing anything.

## Search and filters are the storefront for anyone with intent

A customer who searches converts several times better than one who browses, and a customer who searches and finds nothing usually leaves for good. So zero-result searches are the highest-value report in the business: they are demand you have, expressed in the customer's own words, unmet.

Read them weekly. They tell you what to stock, what to rename and what to redirect.

## A product page answers objections in order

Will it fit me, is it right for my use, what does it really look like, when will it arrive, what if I am wrong. Reviews, dimensions, delivery date and returns policy are not trust decoration — each removes one specific reason not to buy.

Missing delivery dates and missing return terms are the two commonest abandoned-cart causes that have nothing to do with price.

## Range decisions are about cash, not choice

Every SKU consumes working capital, warehouse space and attention. Rank by contribution and rate of sale, then act: the long tail that neither sells nor differentiates is funding nothing.

Deliberate exceptions are fine — a hero item that brings people in, a size that completes a range — but name them as exceptions.

## Watch out for

- Out-of-stock items still indexed and advertised, spending money to disappoint.
- Category pages sorted by "featured" with nobody able to say what that means.
- Discounting used to move stock that a better description would have sold.
- Marketplace listings drifting out of sync with the main catalogue's prices and stock.
- Mobile experience judged on a desktop screen, where most of the traffic is not.

## Finishing

Drop-off is known per funnel step. Zero-result searches are reviewed regularly. Product pages answer fit, use, appearance, delivery and returns. Range decisions state contribution and rate of sale.
