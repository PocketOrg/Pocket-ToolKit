# Structured Hiring

## Define what the person must be able to do, not who they must be

Years of experience and a degree predict performance weakly. A concrete statement of the work — "can take a vague request from a non-technical owner and ship a working page in a week" — predicts it far better and can actually be assessed.

Write that before sourcing. A role definition arrived at by looking at candidates is a definition shaped by who happened to apply.

## The same questions in the same order, or you have no comparison

Unstructured interviews measure rapport. The interviewer feels informed and the signal is close to noise. Structure — same questions, same scoring guide, notes taken during rather than after — is the single largest improvement available.

Score independently before discussing. A group that talks first converges on whoever spoke first, and that is not a decision.

## A work sample beats a conversation about work

Ask for the actual thing, small: review this code, plan this launch, cost this menu. Timeboxed, paid if it is substantial, and the same task for every candidate.

Unpaid multi-day exercises select for people who can afford them, which is not the trait you are hiring for.

## Rejection is part of the product

Every candidate is a potential customer and a source of referrals. Reply, reply reasonably fast, and be specific enough to be useful without being litigable.

Silence after a final-stage interview is the single most-remembered failure of a hiring process.

## Watch out for

- Criteria added mid-process, which almost always rationalises a preference formed earlier.
- "Culture fit" standing in for similarity to the interviewer.
- Referrals skipping the structure — the same bar or a documented exception.
- Salary bands discovered at offer stage after everyone's time is spent.
- Notes that record impressions rather than what the candidate actually said or did.

## Finishing

The role names assessable capabilities. Every candidate got the same questions and an independent score before discussion. Work samples were timeboxed and identical. Everyone received a reply.
