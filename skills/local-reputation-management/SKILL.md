# Local Reputation Management

## Reviews are the storefront for a local business

For anything chosen locally, the rating and the recent reviews are most of the decision, ahead of the website. Volume and recency matter as much as the average — a 4.9 from eleven reviews two years ago reads worse than a 4.5 from two hundred, ongoing.

So the goal is a steady flow, not a high score at one moment.

## Ask everyone, at the right moment, and never buy any

The moment is just after the thing went well, while the person is still present or immediately after. Asked then, plenty of people will. Asked a week later by email, very few.

Never incentivise or fabricate. Platforms detect it, penalties are severe, and a business with obviously bought reviews is worse off than one with none.

## Answer the bad ones for the next reader

The complainant may not come back. The value is in what the next hundred readers see: acknowledged specifically, fixed where fixable, without defensiveness or a legal tone.

One calm, specific reply under a bad review does more for conversion than several good reviews. Arguing does the opposite, permanently.

## Local search is mostly completeness and consistency

Accurate hours including holidays, correct category, photographs that are actually of the place, the same name and address everywhere, and the service list filled in. Unglamorous and it is most of the ranking.

Inconsistent details across directories actively suppress visibility.

## Watch out for

- Review responses written in a legal register, which reads as guilt to every reader.
- Holiday hours never updated, generating the most annoyed reviews of the year.
- One location's reviews dragging a multi-site average with nobody looking per site.
- Photographs that flatter beyond what a visitor will find, which produces disappointment reviews.
- A recurring complaint answered individually many times and never fixed.

## Finishing

Reviews arrive steadily and are asked for at the moment of success. Nothing was incentivised. Negative reviews have calm, specific public replies. Listings are complete and consistent everywhere.
