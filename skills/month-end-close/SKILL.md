# Month-end Close

## A close is a deadline, not a search

The purpose is a set of numbers someone can act on by a known date. A close that produces perfect figures two weeks late has failed at its actual job, because the decisions it was meant to inform were already made.

So work to materiality. An unexplained fifty on a six-figure ledger is noise; chasing it while revenue recognition is unreviewed is the wrong order every time.

## Reconcile from the outside in

Start with the accounts an external party can confirm: bank, merchant processor, payroll provider, tax authority. Those balances are facts. Anything reconciled against your own subledger only proves your ledger agrees with itself.

Every reconciliation names its difference. "Timing" is not an explanation — "three deposits cleared on the 2nd" is.

## Accruals are a policy, not a judgement call

Decide the threshold and the estimation method once, write it down, and apply it. Accruals decided fresh each month become the lever by which the result is quietly managed, whether or not anyone intends that.

An accrual you cannot explain to an auditor next quarter should not be posted this quarter.

## The variance is the deliverable

Nobody reads a balanced trial balance. They read why gross margin moved two points. Investigate the largest movements against both prior month and budget, and state cause rather than restating the number.

If a movement has no cause you can name, say so plainly. An invented explanation is worse than an open question, because it stops anyone looking.

## Watch out for

- Manual journals with no supporting document, especially recurring ones.
- Suspense and clearing accounts that never empty — they hide errors indefinitely.
- Prepayments and deferred revenue amortising on a schedule nobody has checked against the contract.
- A close that depends on one person's spreadsheet and undocumented knowledge.
- Round-number adjustments, which are almost always a plug rather than a calculation.

## Finishing

Every balance-sheet account is reconciled or explicitly explained. Material variances have named causes. The next close could be run by someone else from the notes.
